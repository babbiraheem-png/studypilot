About Us

StudyPilot is a student-focused learning workspace created to make studying feel more organised, focused, and easier to continue every day.

Instead of moving between different tools for questions, revision notes, flashcards, practice, and study progress, StudyPilot brings these learning activities together in one place. The idea is simple: give students a clear starting point, useful study tools, and a workspace they can return to whenever they need to learn or practise.

StudyPilot is designed for students who want to understand concepts, test what they remember, practise questions, and keep track of their learning over time. It supports different school classes and subjects so students can work with a study path that matches what they are learning.

Our approach is centred on active learning. Reading is useful, but learning becomes stronger when students also try questions, explain ideas, recall important information, review difficult topics, and check their understanding. That is why StudyPilot combines revision, practice, flashcards, progress tracking, and an AI Tutor in the same workspace.

We also want the experience to stay simple. Students should be able to open StudyPilot, understand where to go next, and start studying without a complicated setup. The goal is not to overwhelm students with features, but to make useful learning tools easy to reach.

StudyPilot is being developed as a growing student learning platform. As it develops, the focus remains on making the study experience clearer, more useful, and more supportive for students.
